# StyleGuide

## Colors
- `--bg-primary`:     `#0F0F11`   (window backgrounds)
- `--bg-secondary`:   `#17171A`   (card backgrounds)
- `--bg-tertiary`:    `#1E1E22`   (input fields, hover states)
- `--bg-sidebar`:     `#0C0C0E`   (sidebar)
- `--accent`:         `#FF6B35`   (saffron — CTAs, active states)
- `--accent-soft`:    `#FF6B3520` (accent at 12% opacity — active bg)
- `--text-primary`:   `#F2F2F3`
- `--text-secondary`: `#8A8A96`
- `--text-tertiary`:  `#55555F`
- `--separator`:      `#2A2A30`
- `--success`:        `#34C759`
- `--error`:          `#FF453A`
- `--warning`:        `#FFD60A`

## Typography (SF Pro)
- `--title`:    SF Pro Display, 15pt, semibold
- `--body`:     SF Pro Text, 13pt, regular
- `--label`:    SF Pro Text, 11pt, medium, letter-spacing +0.2
- `--caption`:  SF Pro Text, 10pt, regular
- `--mono`:     SF Mono, 12pt, regular

## Spacing & Grid
- **Spacing:** 4 / 8 / 12 / 16 / 24 / 32pt grid
- **Corner radius:** 6pt (inputs), 8pt (cards), 12pt (modals), 26pt (pill)
- **Shadows:** `0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.4)`, `0 8px 24px rgba(0,0,0,0.3)`
